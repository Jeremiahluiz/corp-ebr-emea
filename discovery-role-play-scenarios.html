Scenario 02 · Platform consolidation

Vodafone · Mixed landscape and global standardization

Explore how a Microsoft-heavy enterprise balances GitHub Enterprise, Azure DevOps, and legacy developer platforms across distributed teams.

C
Persona

Chief Technology Officer

Balances delivery speed, platform economics, governance, security, and developer experience.

Customer

Vodafone

Vodafone is a European telecommunications company with more than 9,000 developers across 12 countries. Its teams historically selected their own tools, including GitHub, Bitbucket, and GitLab.

Vodafone standardized repositories on GitHub Enterprise while retaining Microsoft Azure DevOps capabilities for areas such as project management and deployment. This makes it a strong scenario for discussing fragmentation, platform roles, and global collaboration.
