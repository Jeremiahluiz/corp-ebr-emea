Q1 lessons · Strengths and opportunities

Build on what worked. Focus improvement where it can create the biggest lift.

What worked well
AE collaboration and feedback

Collaboration improved significantly across the quarter, with further opportunity to strengthen the partnership.

Planning and forecasting discipline

Stronger planning and forecasting helped the team overachieve in September.

Learning and calling sessions

Regular sessions built skills, created shared momentum, and made calling more enjoyable.

Peer support accelerated onboarding

Buddies and experienced EBRs invested time in helping Renato, Renata, Anna, Tommy, and Illane ramp into new or newly promoted EBR roles. Their impact deserves recognition.

Adaptability through change

The team embraced a new narrative, new ways of selling, new leadership, and a significant wave of onboarding.

Opportunities for improvement

Where can we create the next lift?

Increase urgency on low-hanging fruit

Act faster on Account Recommender signals and inbound leads while customer intent is high.

Strengthen sales acumen and seller workflow

Make calling and LinkedIn consistent parts of our daily prospecting activity.

Raise prospecting quality

Improve qualification, messaging, and our overall expertise in the Sales Development discipline.

Carry the strengths forward and make the improvement areas actionable04
