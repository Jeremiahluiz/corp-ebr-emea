        Practice focus

        Opening readiness

        The goal is not to pitch or force a meeting. Earn enough relevance to start a useful customer conversation.

        • Recognize what the customer already uses
        • Use one specific, plausible gap
        • Stay neutral and easy to disagree with
        • End with a specific “How do you know?” question
        • Deliver slowly enough to sound conversational

        If they engage

        Light SPIN follow-ups

        Do not turn the opener into a full discovery call. Ask one relevant follow-up, listen, and agree a customer-led next step only when useful.

        Cold-call approach and sources

        This builder synthesizes public guidance from Josh Braun and Benjamin Dennehy for practice. The default questions follow Braun’s “poke the bear” principle: surface a specific risk the customer may not have considered, avoid a leading answer, and let them examine how they know the current approach is working. Dennehy’s emphasis on brevity, confidence, and practice informs the delivery coaching. The wording in this app is original and configurable.
